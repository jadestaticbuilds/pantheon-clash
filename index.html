PANTHEON
CLASH

Eighteen gods. Three pantheons. One arena.

How to play
  • Your deck holds gods only. No magic cards. Ever.
  • Draw 6 gods. Crown one as your patron: it does not fight, but gods sharing its trait gain +1 Power all match.
  • Each match has an arena: Olympus favors Courage, Alexandria favors Wisdom, Elysian Fields favors Kindness (+1 Power, both sides).
  • Five rounds. Each round both sides play one god, revealed together.
  • The triangle: Wisdom outthinks Courage, Courage overwhelms Kindness, Kindness disarms Wisdom (+2 Power).
  • Higher total wins the round. Damage equals the difference. Ties wound both for 1.
  • Both sides have 25 life. At zero, the match ends. After five rounds, higher life wins. A tie means sudden death.
  • Every god has one ability. Read them. Use them. The gods reward the cunning.
